# SoulCityGame

Unreal Engine 5.8 C++ co-op shooter prototype. Players share a team and fight waves of Murdock bots in a Soul: City rooftop arena.

## Play

Open `SoulCityGame.uproject` and press Play. The default map is `/Game/SoulCityGame/Maps/L_CityCombat` and the game mode is `/Script/CityCombat.CityGameMode`. The arena now takes place in the original Soul City streets.

- WASD: move; mouse: camera; Shift: sprint; Space: jump or vault a nearby suitable fence.
- E: collect a nearby rifle or reserve ammunition. The prompt appears within 2.25 m with a clear line of sight.
- Left mouse: fire; hold right mouse for the 4x scope; V toggles the scope; R: reload. Scope sensitivity is reduced and reloading temporarily leaves the scope view.
- Rifle: 30-round magazine, 90 spare rounds per pickup, 1.8-second reload. Empty magazines reload automatically while firing.
- F6: Soul City combat; F7: Soul City exploration/parkour without waves. The host controls map changes and connected clients follow.
- H: return to the last safe position, with a five-second cooldown. Health and ammunition are preserved. After a large drop, disconnected walkable pockets also trigger automatic recovery.
- F5: restart the current map as a LAN listen server. Another game instance can connect using the console command `JoinCity <host LAN IP>:7777`.

`L_CityCombat` uses Soul City's actual streets, a two-player staging area at the original city entrance and 28 distant enemy sectors. Enemy spawns are checked against every living player and must be at least 26 metres away. The HUD shows the nearest enemy's distance and whether it is ahead or behind. `L_CityOpen` shares the city geometry and the new 45 x 60 m western district, connected by a six-metre-wide promenade. There are four accessible roofs, two staircase approaches, a bridge, a sprint-jump gap and three fence obstacles. Both versions include an escape staircase in the low grated service pit. The former separate `L_CityArena` rooftop map is preserved as an archive and is no longer selected by the normal controls. Building interiors are not implemented.

Small obstacles up to 55 cm can be stepped over; jumping is limited to one jump of approximately 90 cm on level ground. Space near a thin 60-145 cm obstacle vaults it, or climbs onto a clear ledge up to 180 cm high. The server validates the overhead path and landing and synchronizes the traversal. Higher walls and blocked landings are rejected. Falling below either map kills the character. Players respawn after five seconds and have five seconds of protection from enemy damage.

The shared HUD shows rounds won, current wave, surviving bots and the next-wave countdown. The team panel shows connected players, health, kills and deaths. Friendly fire is disabled. In combat mode, waves begin after six seconds; clearing a wave awards one round and starts an eight-second break. Bot count grows from three to a maximum of eight. Exploration mode has no wave timer.

## Implementation

`Source/CityCombat/CityCombatActors.*` implements replicated movement/combat state, server-authoritative pickup, ammunition, damage, reload, vaulting, bot navigation and respawning. Bots use Unreal navigation with local steering as a fallback. The locomotion blend space follows actual movement speed and direction. The equipment marker on the ground is extracted from the supplied Murdock weapon mesh; equipping reveals the arm cannon and its added metal optic with a blue lens. The 4x view uses a 28-degree field of view, circular housing mask, mil marks and a red centre dot. Hits use a camera target followed by a muzzle trace to respect nearby cover.

The weapons now have spatial shot and reload sounds, visible blue/red plasma tracers, supplied Paragon muzzle/impact effects and recoil. Three original synthesized shot samples and a mechanical reload sample are generated by `Scripts/build_combat_audio.py`; their WAV sources live in `SourceAssets/Audio`. Metal footsteps come from Soul City. Fire, moving fire, stationary/moving reload, jump takeoff, airborne, landing, traversal and death animations are connected. This remains a gameplay prototype; online matchmaking and a complete layered animation state machine are future work.

## Build and checks

Build `SoulCityGameEditor Win64 Development` using UE 5.8's `Engine/Build/BatchFiles/Build.bat` with `-Project=<absolute path to SoulCityGame.uproject> -WaitMutex -NoHotReloadFromIDE`. Close running editor/game instances before rebuilding the module.

Automation test: run `CityCombat.Gameplay.AuthorityRules` in Session Frontend, or use `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <project> -NullRHI -unattended "-ExecCmds=Automation RunTests CityCombat.Gameplay" "-TestExit=Automation Test Queue Empty"`. It checks pickup distance, duplicate collection, ammunition conservation, fire-rate enforcement, reloading, team damage and enemy death.

Development-only smoke flags:

- `-CityArenaTest`: automatically checks pickup, a 35 cm step, bot movement, clearing a wave and falling off the map; exits after 15 seconds of gameplay. Logs `CITY_ARENA_*` and `CITY_BOT_MOVEMENT`.
- `-CityNetTest`: disables waves for a network smoke test. Clients pick up, fire, reload, report replicated state and exit. The listen server exits after 45 seconds. Use separate server/client log paths and `-NullRHI -nosound` for headless checks.
- `-CityVisualTest`: a 90-second visual review without bot waves; automatically picks up at eight seconds, aims at 15 seconds, then fires and reloads at 75 seconds.
- `-CityParkourTest`: on either map, checks fence detection, completing a vault, rejecting a high wall, 4x field of view and leaving the scope during reload; exits after eight seconds.
- `-CityConnectivityTest`: on `L_CityOpen`, walks down the promenade into the original streets; exits after ten seconds.
- `-CityMapTravelTest`: start in the arena; travels to Soul City and back, then exits.
- `-CityVaultNetTest` alongside `-CityNetTest`: adds synchronized fence vaulting and scope activation to the two-client network smoke test.
- `-CityBattleTest` on `L_CityCombat`: checks loaded sound/animation/FX assets, actual bot waves and movement, ammunition/reload, round scoring and recovery; exits after 14 seconds.
- `-CityPitTest`: walks up the service-pit escape staircase, then over the connecting bridge to the street; exits after 7.5 seconds.

## Assets and map generation

- Environment: [Soul: City — Epic Games](https://www.fab.com/listings/dd77fee6-0ad2-41ce-b32c-09300c24c9f3)
- Character and weapon: [Paragon: Murdock — Epic Games](https://www.fab.com/listings/89ae5876-3350-432e-882b-7d617e8c7e6f)

The assets are imported under `Content/SoulCity` and `Content/ParagonMurdock`. Fab's store plugin is disabled in this project because its locally updated version failed module discovery; imported content does not depend on that plugin.

`Scripts/build_combat_audio.py` generates/imports the weapon samples and creates missing optic/tracer materials. `Scripts/build_open_city.py` recreates the open-city copy from `L_CityPrototype`, clears the western connector, decorative obstruction and demo blocking volumes, and adds the new district. `Scripts/build_city_combat.py` adds the pit exit to both city variants and builds the combat copy with validated distant enemy sectors. These scripts overwrite generated maps, so preserve manual edits before rerunning them. `parkour_geometry.py` supplies the shared railings and pit staircase. `Scripts/build_arena.py` belongs to the archived rooftop map. `Scripts/build_weapon.py` extracts the equipment marker mesh if missing. Equipped characters use the integrated arm cannon. These scripts require the compiled editor module and imported packs. Original asset-pack maps are preserved.

Keep third-party asset license records with the project and check the applicable terms before distribution. Do not publish the raw Fab content as a public asset repository. Paragon branding is not the game's title.
